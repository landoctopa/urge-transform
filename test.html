Foundations of a
studio.

A useful resource doesn't need to tell people exactly what to do.

Sometimes its job is simply to help them see something differently.

Start with what is real.

Good work begins by paying attention to what already exists: what people do, what they struggle with and where there is friction.

Don't build the answer before understanding the question.

Use what you learn to decide what deserves your attention next.
