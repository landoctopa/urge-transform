Mission 01 · Getting Started

Start before
you are ready.

Most people think starting a business begins with a great idea.

It doesn't.

It begins when you notice something that could be better and become curious enough to investigate what is actually happening.

The goal isn't certainty.

The goal is to learn something real. You are going to investigate, act, observe and reflect.

Start with what is real.

Pay attention to what people actually do, what frustrates them and what they already spend time, money or effort trying to solve.
